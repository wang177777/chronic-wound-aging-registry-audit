# Publication figure rendering specification

The journal-facing figures are presentation-only displays of the frozen CSVs in `publication/`; they do not introduce a new endpoint, denominator, subgroup, model or inferential result.

- Maximum exported width: 183 mm; maximum exported height: 170 mm.
- Panel labels: 8 pt bold sans serif. Other final-size text: approximately 5.2–7 pt sans serif.
- Raster deliverables: 600 dpi. Editable PDF and SVG counterparts are retained in the submission package.
- Figure 1 uses separated rounded nodes and orthogonal connectors. The reliability and full-screening branches merge only at the final cohort.
- Figure 3 percentage ticks end at 100%. Additional horizontal space in panel a is annotation space for specified and indeterminate counts, not an extension of the percentage scale.
- Figure 4 uses a common 0–50% horizontal scale across all four panels.
- Supplementary Figure S1 percentages use the stated full denominators; the displayed cells in panels b and c therefore do not sum to 100%.

These rules affect presentation only. The source CSV hashes and all validated scientific outputs are unchanged from v1.2.10.
