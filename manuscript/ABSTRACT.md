# Abstract

Older adults with chronic wounds may be formally eligible for trials while geriatric complexity and patient-important outcomes remain incompletely specified. We audited 1,218 eligible ClinicalTrials.gov records first posted in 2008–2025 using hash-verified frozen JSON. Reconciled eligibility at age 85 was documented for 858 records (70.44%), at least one of eight geriatric-context domains for 575 (47.21%), and both features for 392 (32.18%). In venous-leg-ulcer active-treatment trials, complete any-registered CoreVen coverage occurred in 2 of 304 records; in pressure-injury-prevention trials, complete OUTPUTs coverage occurred in 0 of 184. Domain-level coverage varied substantially and indeterminate states were common. These registry-level findings distinguish formal age access from public specification of geriatric context and registered outcome coverage; they do not establish actual enrollment, participant representation, prospective outcome specification, or core-outcome-set adoption.
